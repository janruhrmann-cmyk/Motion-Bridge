Looking for After Effects…

Open Window › Extensions › Motion Bridge in After Effects.

What is Motion Bridge?

It imports your After Effects animations into Figma Motion automatically. Send a composition from the After Effects panel, or pull the one that is open there (the second tile), and it is built here as a frame, motion included. When the composition changes later, Update designs rebuilds the frame where it is.

The other way round, Push to After Effects (the first tile) turns the frames you select into compositions: layers, text, pictures and Figma Motion keyframes.
